# Omnave: Product Vision

**The Core Identity:** Omnave is the ultimate AI study companion for students. 
**The Vibe:** It should feel like a premium, distraction-free workspace (like a high-end physical notebook or a beautifully lit desk), not a boring school utility.

**Our Core Directives:**
1. **Speed is a Feature:** Interactions must feel instantaneous. No clunky page loads.
2. **Offline-Ready:** Students need to study anywhere, regardless of data limits.
3. **Respect the User's Focus:** The material is the hero. The UI gets out of the way.
