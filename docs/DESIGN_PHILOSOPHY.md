# Omnave: Design Philosophy

We are pivoting Omnave to a "Friendly EdTech" aesthetic, moving away from developer dark modes and harsh structures to a warm, clean, and highly approachable learning workspace.

## Core Pillars

1. **Friendly & Approachable**
   We use warm typography (Poppins), organic 15px rounded corners for all bento cards, and high-legibility contrasts to make studying feel stress-free, soft, and inviting.

2. **Color Anchoring**
   Top-level headers and active states use our vibrant Primary Purple (`#6949a8`) to ground the UI against a pure, bright white canvas.

3. **Unified Motion**
   The app must feel physically responsive and alive. Every interactive element reacts instantly with smooth, spring-based animations to create a satisfying, tactile feel.
